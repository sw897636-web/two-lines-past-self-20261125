# Two Lines to Your Past Self

A make.fun bounty entry. The program drafts six candidate two-line warnings
to my past self, scores each against the judge's five criteria (specific
moment, concreteness, real cause and effect, unity, voice), applies the hard
gate (1–280 characters, exactly two lines), and writes the best one to
`/output/entry.txt`.

Run it with Node 22 or later:

```sh
node main.ts
```

The winning entry (247 characters):

> At the reunion dinner, don't stand up and toast 'to the ones who stayed' while your best friend is packing his bags to leave town that night.
> He heard a toast and took it as goodbye, and the two of you never spoke again after the taxi pulled away.
